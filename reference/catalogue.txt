ABOUT US
INOX, Established in the year 2000 in India with the introduction of the 
kitchen accessories. Our reputation for incomparable quality products 
is well established across the continent. After specializing in kitchen 
accessories, INOX introduced stimulating and practical ideas of space 
management for wardrobes and bathrooms which have now, become 
an integral part of every home, further INOX expanded into the field of 
powder coating, delivering the best results to the customers through 
its art powder coating and wood effect coating facility. We have the 
widest range of wood finish patterns and also a wide range of RAL 
colors for interior and exterior applications - textured and smooth 
finishes - smooth matte - smooth semi-glossy - fine texture - rough 
texture matte - rough texture glossy - metallic, copper, brass and 
chrome finish etc. in Polyester epoxy, Polyester, PolyUrethane and 
Epoxy. Our operational procedures meet the requirement of ISO 
9001:2015, quality management systems for which we continue to 
strive to improve our process. The quality and performance of our 
coated objects are tested at regular intervals. We adhere strictly to the 
standards as laid down and supported by in-depth quality control 
process. We are committed to understanding needs and expectations 
of customers and enhancing value through mutually beneficial 
customer / supplier relationship. At INOX, our non-compromising 
attitude towards quality standards whether in materials, form, function 
and desire for innovative and new designs has built the competence 
and trust in the international markets today. With our network of 
dealers and distributors and our ambitious plans for expansion, we are 
dedicated to deliver excellence in quality products, services and 
satisfaction. It is hence imperative on our part to always take a 
proactive stance in the direction of strengthening our relationship and 
forging ahead towards excellence.
